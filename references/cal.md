# Cal / UC Berkeley Citation Format

Source: `Cal Formatting Guide.docx` (Formatting Guide - UC Berkeley - 2026). Citation rules only; card cutting, file organization, and analytics rules live in the original docx.

## Template

```
**Last YY** — First Last. Month Day. Qualifications. Publication, “Title,” URL
```

| Slot | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Oral cite | `**Last YY**`: first author's last name + two-digit year, no apostrophe. Bold (Verbatim "Cite" style). Keep name particles as printed (`van Hiel 24`). Nothing else in the citation is formatted. |
| Separator | space, em dash (U+2014 `—`), space. This em dash is part of the format; never replace it with a hyphen. |
| Authors | Full name(s) of every listed author, ending with a period. |
| Date | `Month Day.` (e.g. `April 11.`). The year is NOT repeated here; it is in the oral cite. Day unknown: `Month.` Only the year known: `YYYY.` |
| Qualifications | One segment per author, in author order. Each segment ends with a period. Items within a segment are comma-separated. |
| Publication | Outlet name, followed by a comma. |
| Title | Curly double quotes, comma inside the closing quote: `“Title,”` |
| URL | Sanitized (strip tracking params like `utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`). No trailing period after the URL. |

Quotes are curly (`“ ”`, `’`), not straight.

### Example

```
**Hirsh 25** — Michael Hirsh. April 11. Former foreign editor and chief diplomatic correspondent for Newsweek, and the former national editor for Politico Magazine. Politico, “Trump May Be Triggering the Fastest Nuclear Weapons Race Since the Cold War,” https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2025/04/11/trump-says-he-fears-nuclear-weapons-so-why-is-he-making-them-more-popular-00278790
```

## Multiple Authors

- Oral cite uses only the first author's last name.
- Two authors: `First Last and First Last.`
- Three or more: `First Last, First Last, and First Last.` (serial comma).
- Large author lists may name the first author(s) and end with `, et. al.` (written exactly `et. al.`). Give qualifications only for named authors.
- Add one qualification segment per named author, in the same order as the names.

```
**Keith 25** — Douglas Keith and Yasmin Abusaif. February 14. Senior counsel in the Brennan Center’s Judiciary Program, JD from NYU School of Law. Counsel in the Brennan Center’s Elections and Government Program, JD from UCLA School of Law. Brennan Center for Justice, “What Courts Can Do If the Trump Administration Defies Court Orders,” https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/what-courts-can-do-if-trump-administration-defies-court-orders
```

```
**Schmitz 23** — Oswald J. Schmitz, Magnus Sylvén, and Trisha B. Atwood. 2023. Professor of Population and Community Ecology in the Yale School of the Environment, studying biodiversity and ecosystem services, PhD from the University of Michigan. PhD in Animal Ecology from Lund University, Director of the Global Rewilding Alliance. Associate Professor of Ecology at Utah State University, PhD in Ecology from the University of British Columbia. Nature Climate Change, “Trophic Rewilding Can Expand Natural Climate Solutions,” vol. 13
```

```
**Haynes 25** — Abby Haynes, Catherine Sherrington, et. al. 2025. Research Fellow at the Institute for Musculoskeletal Health, University of Sydney, PhD. Professor, Sydney School of Public Health, University of Sydney, Deputy Director of the Institute for Musculoskeletal Health. The International Journal of Sport and Society, “Increasing Meaningful Participation of Children with Disability in Little Athletics: Development of a More Inclusive Competition Structure,” https://doi.org/10.18848/2152-7857/CGP/v16i02/75-102
```

## Time Sensitive

Use only when the evidence was published within one month of today's date (this skill's threshold; the original guide says two months).

- Oral cite: `**Last M-D**`: month and day numbers joined by a hyphen, no leading zeros, no year.
- Date slot in the body becomes the year: `YYYY.`
- Everything else is unchanged.

```
**Rubin 1-7** — Richard Rubin. 2025. US tax policy reporter for The Wall Street Journal. WSJ, “Debt-Ceiling Fight Has New X Factor: Trump,” https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/us-congress-debt-limit-trump-2c45575d
```

## Journal

1. URL should be authoritative, most commonly the DOI (`https://doi.org/...`). Sanitize it.
2. No authoritative URL: replace the URL with the volume or page numbers. Neither available: use the issue number.
3. Abbreviations: volume `vol. 35`, number `no. 3`, pages `p. 65-67`.

```
**van Hiel 24** — Alain van Hiel, et. al. 2024. Professor of Psychology at Ghent University (Belgium). European Review of Social Psychology, “Procedural Fairness in Ethnic-Cultural Decision-Making: Benefits, Processes and Minority and Majority Group Perspectives,” vol. 35
```

```
**Eichensehr 21** — Kristen E. Eichensehr. March. Professor of Law at the University of Virginia School of Law, Visiting Professor of Law at Harvard Law School, member of the U.S. State Department’s Advisory Committee on International Law. Duke Law Journal, “The Youngstown Canon: Vetoed Bills and the Separation of Powers,” vol. 70
```

## Book

1. Publication = the book's title. Failing that, the publisher (e.g. Oxford Press).
2. Title = the chapter being cut.
3. URL is replaced by the chapter (`Ch. 4`) or pages (`p. 65-67`).

Chapter:

```
**Fitzpatrick 16** — Mark Fitzpatrick. February 3. Associate Fellow and former Executive Director of the International Institute for Strategic Studies, former Deputy Assistant Secretary for Non-Proliferation at the Department of State, M.A. in Public Policy from Harvard University. Asia’s Latent Nuclear Powers, “Taiwan,” Ch. 3
```

Pages:

```
**Cribb 17** — Julian Cribb. 2017. Principal of Julian Cribb & Associates, Fellow of the Australian Academy of Technological Sciences and Engineering, former Director of National Awareness at the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation. Surviving the 21st Century, “The Baker,” p. 91-93
```

Publisher as publication, book title as title (no chapter or pages known):

```
**Koh 24** — Harold Hongju Koh. 2024. Sterling Professor of International Law, Yale Law School, Legal Adviser to the U.S. Department of State, Assistant Secretary of State for Democracy, Human Rights, and Labor. Yale University Press, “The National Security Constitution in the Twenty-First Century”
```

## Court Case

The author is the judge who wrote the opinion, never "the Court".

1. Qualifications = the judge's position (plus relevant background).
2. Publication = the court that decided the case.
3. Title = the case name, in quotes.
4. URL is replaced by the court reporter citation.

```
**Harlan 56** — John Marshall Harlan. 1956. Associate Justice of the SCOTUS. Supreme Court of the United States, “De Sylva v. Ballentine,” 351 U.S. 570
```

```
**Lyons 99** — Champ Lyons. 1999. Justice of the Supreme Court of Alabama, BA from Harvard, JD from Alabama School of Law. Supreme Court of Alabama, “Ex parte A-O Mach. Co.,” 749 So. 2d 1268
```

```
**Black 52** — Hugo Black. 1952. US Senator, Associate Justice of the SCOTUS. Supreme Court of the United States, “Youngstown Sheet & Tube Co. v. Sawyer,” 343 U.S. 579
```
