# CME / Lynbrook Citation Format

Source: `Lynbrook Formatting Guide.docx` (Formatting Guide - Lynbrook - 2026). Citation rules only; card cutting, file organization, and analytics rules live in the original docx.

## Template

```
First **Last ’YY**. Qualifications. “Title.” Publication. Month Day, YYYY. URL.
```

| Slot | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Name + oral cite | First (and middle) name unformatted, then `**Last ’YY**` in bold (Verbatim "Cite" style): last name, space, right single quote (U+2019 `’`), two-digit year. Nothing else in the citation is formatted. |
| Qualifications | Comma-separated items, ending with a period. |
| Title | Curly double quotes, period inside the closing quote: `“Title.”` |
| Publication | Outlet name, ending with a period. |
| Date | Full date: `April 28, 2025.` Day unknown: `March 2021.` Only the year known: `2024.` |
| URL | Sanitized (strip tracking params like `utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`), followed by a period. |

Every citation ends with a period. Quotes are curly (`“ ”`, `’`), not straight.

### Example

```
Michael **Hirsh ’25**. Former foreign editor and chief diplomatic correspondent for Newsweek, and the former national editor for Politico Magazine. “Trump May Be Triggering the Fastest Nuclear Weapons Race Since the Cold War.” Politico. April 11, 2025. https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2025/04/11/trump-says-he-fears-nuclear-weapons-so-why-is-he-making-them-more-popular-00278790.
```

## Multiple Authors

Only the first author is bolded with the year. Each additional author is `First Last. Qualifications.`, unformatted.

### Two authors: one line

Second author and qualifications go right after the first author's qualifications.

```
Douglas **Keith ’25**. Senior counsel in the Brennan Center’s Judiciary Program, JD from NYU School of Law. Yasmin Abusaif. Counsel in the Brennan Center’s Elections and Government Program, JD from UCLA School of Law. “What Courts Can Do If the Trump Administration Defies Court Orders.” Brennan Center for Justice. February 14, 2025. https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/what-courts-can-do-if-trump-administration-defies-court-orders.
```

### Three or more authors: one line per author

- Each author and their qualifications is its own line (paragraph).
- The `“Title.” Publication. Date. URL.` portion is its own final line.
- List authors in source order, up to five. If the source has more authors than you list, add a line containing only `et. al.` (written exactly that way) before the title line.

```
Oswald J. **Schmitz ’23**. Professor of Population and Community Ecology in the Yale School of the Environment, studying biodiversity and ecosystem services, PhD from the University of Michigan.
Magnus Sylvén. PhD in Animal Ecology from Lund University, Director of the Global Rewilding Alliance.
Trisha B. Atwood. Associate Professor of Ecology at Utah State University, PhD in Ecology from the University of British Columbia.
et. al.
“Trophic Rewilding Can Expand Natural Climate Solutions.” Nature Climate Change, vol. 13, no. 4, p. 324-33. 2023. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41558-023-01631-6.
```

```
David **Winks ’25**. Senior advisor for Advanced Technology, previously at the DHS Resilient Power Working Group, subject matter expert at the DOD.
Steve Chill. Retired marine with experience in security, created DOD policy.
Frederick Ferrer. Intelligence professional with 20-year career in US intelligence, PhD.
Steve Cohan. Officer in the Coast Guard.
et. al.
“Cost Analysis: Protecting the Grid and Electronics from an EMP.” Domestic Preparedness. March 12, 2025. https://www.domesticpreparedness.com/articles/cost-analysis-protecting-the-grid-and-electronics-from-an-emp.
```

## Time Sensitive

Use only when the evidence was published within one month of today's date (this skill's threshold; the original guide says two months).

- Oral cite: `**Last ’M-D**`: month and day numbers joined by a hyphen, no leading zeros, no year.
- The body keeps the full date (`January 7, 2025.`).

```
Richard **Rubin ’1-7**. US tax policy reporter for The Wall Street Journal. “Debt-Ceiling Fight Has New X Factor: Trump.” WSJ. January 7, 2025. https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/us-congress-debt-limit-trump-2c45575d.
```

## Journal

1. Include an authoritative URL when available, most commonly the DOI (`https://doi.org/...`). Sanitize it.
2. Publication = journal name plus identifying info, comma-separated: volume `vol. 35`, number `no. 2`, pages `p. 65-67`.
3. Date is often just the year or month and year.

```
Alain **Van Hiel ’24**. Professor of Psychology at Ghent University (Belgium). “Procedural Fairness in Ethnic-Cultural Decision-Making: Benefits, Processes and Minority and Majority Group Perspectives.” European Review of Social Psychology, vol. 35, no. 2. 2024.
```

```
Abby **Haynes ’25**. Research Fellow at the Institute for Musculoskeletal Health, University of Sydney, PhD. “Increasing Meaningful Participation of Children with Disability in Little Athletics.” The International Journal of Sport and Society. 2025. https://doi.org/10.18848/2152-7857/CGP/v16i02/75-102.
```

```
Kristen E. **Eichensehr ’21**. Professor of Law at the University of Virginia School of Law, Visiting Professor of Law at Harvard Law School, member of the U.S. State Department’s Advisory Committee on International Law. “The Youngstown Canon: Vetoed Bills and the Separation of Powers.” Duke Law Journal, vol. 70. March 2021.
```

## Book

1. Publication = the book's title. Failing that, the publisher (e.g. Oxford Press).
2. Title = the chapter being cut.
3. URL is replaced by the chapter (`Ch. 4`) or pages (`p. 65-67`).

```
Laurence R. **Helfer ’20**. “Terminating Treaties.” The Oxford Guide to Treaties. 2020. p. 48.
```

Publisher as publication, book title as title:

```
Harold Hongju **Koh ’24**. Sterling Professor of International Law, Yale Law School, Legal Adviser to the U.S. Department of State, Assistant Secretary of State for Democracy, Human Rights, and Labor. “The National Security Constitution in the Twenty-First Century.” Yale University Press. 2024. Ch. 4.
```

## Court Case

The author is the judge who wrote the opinion, never "the Court". Include the court reporter citation when available.

```
First **Last ’YY**. Qualifications. Case Name. Reporter Citation, Court. YYYY.
```

The case name is NOT quoted.

```
John Marshall **Harlan ’56**. Associate Justice of the SCOTUS. De Sylva v. Ballentine. 351 U.S. 570, Supreme Court of the United States. 1956.
```

```
Champ **Lyons ’99**. Justice of the Supreme Court of Alabama, BA from Harvard, JD from Alabama School of Law. Ex parte A-O Mach. Co. 749 So. 2d 1268, Supreme Court of Alabama. 1999.
```

```
Hugo **Black ’52**. US Senator, Associate Justice of the SCOTUS. Youngstown Sheet & Tube Co. v. Sawyer. 343 U.S. 579, Supreme Court of the United States. 1952.
```
